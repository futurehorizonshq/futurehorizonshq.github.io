Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Future Horizons ("we", "us") publishes content on social media platforms and uses an internal publishing tool, Future Horizons Studio, to schedule and post our own content to accounts we own.

What we collect

This website does not ask you for personal information and does not use tracking cookies. If you email us, we receive your email address and message, used only to reply. Our publishing tool connects only to Future Horizons' own social media accounts through the official APIs of TikTok, Meta (Instagram, Facebook, Threads) and YouTube. It accesses basic account information (such as account ID, username and avatar), our own posts and their statistics, solely to publish and measure our own content.

TikTok data

When our tool connects to our TikTok account it uses TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API with the scopes needed to read our basic profile information and to upload and publish our own videos. We do not access other users' data, and we do not share TikTok data with anyone.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal data. We do not collect data from other users' accounts. We do not use platform data to build advertising profiles.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored privately on a secured device, are never shared, and can be revoked at any time from the platform's settings. Data is kept only as long as needed to run our own accounts.

Third-party platforms

When you use YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, Threads or TikTok, their own privacy policies apply.

Your rights (UK GDPR)

You may request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal data by emailing futurehorizonshq@gmail.com. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
